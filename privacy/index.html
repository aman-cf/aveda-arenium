Who we are

  • Details you give us in a form: name, mobile number, email, city, and anything you write in a message, including job applications, partner registrations and land proposals.

Your consent

We process your personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you tick the box on our forms, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You may withdraw consent at any time by writing to us; this does not affect processing already carried out.

Who we share it with

Your details are shared only with DKG staff and service providers who help us respond (for example our CRM, email and messaging providers), under confidentiality. We do not sell your personal data.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and job applications for up to 12 months, unless the law requires longer.
