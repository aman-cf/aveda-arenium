1.0 — Our legacy

Guided by the enduring legacy of DMR.

With a legacy of over 25 years, DMR Builders has been instrumental in building the nation's backbone. Founded by the late Mr. Darshan Garg, the group has crafted landmark structures through tunnels, bridges, highways and flyovers that have become integral to the nation's continual growth and movement.

The group has collaborated with various departments and authorities, including the Punjab Government, the National Highways Authority of India and numerous district corporations, using modern construction technology and engineering excellence to deliver world-class infrastructure that endures the test of time.
