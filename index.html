7.0 — The place

Rooted in the corridor.

On PR-7, the airport road through GMADA Aerocity: five minutes from Chandigarh International Airport and a short drive from IT City, ISB and the Tricity's hospitals.

    7.1 — Why now

    A decade-long constraint, being resolved.

    2015The bottleneck begins

    The airport's new terminal opens and the convenient domestic terminal closes, removing the short route from Chandigarh, Panchkula and Zirakpur.

    2025State-level approval

    A shorter airport link for Zirakpur, Panchkula and the Kalka–Shimla corridor is approved, cutting the route from 13.7 km to 9.6 km.

    2026Parallel fixes under way

    NHAI awards the Zirakpur–Panchkula bypass and a spur to the Ambala–Chandigarh Expressway; PR-7 widening moves into construction.

    2027+The corridor matures

    Apollo's 736-bed hospital targets completion and Infosys's new campus scales toward full occupancy.
