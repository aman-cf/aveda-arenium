1.0 — Our legacy

Guided by the enduring legacy of DMR.

For more than 25 years, DMR Builders has helped build the nation's backbone: tunnels, bridges, highways and flyovers that carry Punjab's daily movement. Founded by the late Mr. Darshan Garg, the group has worked with the Punjab Government, the National Highways Authority of India and district corporations across the state.

DKG Landcorp carries that legacy into real estate, with the same engineering discipline behind every home.
